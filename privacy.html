Privacy Policy

Effective · September 27, 2026Last updated · September 27, 2026

WorkTime does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device, under your control.

01Who we are

WorkTime is a free time-tracking app for Android, developed and published by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). This policy applies to the WorkTime app and to this website, kirisinclair.github.io/Worktime.

02What data the app handles

To work, the app stores the information you enter yourself:

  • areas, projects and tasks, with their names and colours;
  • work sessions and breaks: start and end time, duration and the project or task they belong to;
  • settings such as your daily goal, sleep time for Absolute Time, theme and hidden or archived items.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or any other personal identifiers. The app has no user accounts.

03Where your data is stored

All data is stored locally in the app’s private storage on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers or to any cloud service, and we have no access to it.

If Android’s system backup is enabled on your device, Android may include app data in your personal Google device backup. This is controlled by your device settings and Google’s own privacy policy, not by us.

04Backups and CSV files

When you create a backup or export a CSV file, the app saves it to a location you pick using the standard Android file picker. From that moment you decide where the file goes and who can see it. The app reads a file only when you choose to restore a backup or import a CSV.

Exported files contain your tracked time in plain form. Treat them like any other personal document.

05Permissions

The app asks only for permissions needed for its features. Depending on your Android version, it may ask to show notifications so a running timer and break reminders stay visible. You can revoke permissions at any time in your device settings. The app does not request location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

06Analytics, ads and third parties

WorkTime contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking SDKs. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, because we do not receive any data in the first place.

If you download the app from Google Play, Google may collect information about the installation under its own privacy policy. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes. This website does not use cookies or analytics. The theme you choose is remembered in your browser’s local storage only.

07Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can edit or delete individual sessions, archive or remove areas, projects and tasks, or clear the app’s data in Android settings. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored by it. Backup and CSV files you saved elsewhere are not affected and can be deleted by you.

08Security

Your data is protected by Android’s app sandbox, which prevents other apps from reading WorkTime’s private storage. Keeping your device locked and your exported files in safe places adds another layer of protection.

09Children

WorkTime is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

10Your rights

Because we do not hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You have full control over your data directly in the app. Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or CCPA may give you additional rights; if you have questions about them, contact us.

11Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.

12Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised through GitHub Issues in the project repository: github.com/kirisinclair/Worktime.
